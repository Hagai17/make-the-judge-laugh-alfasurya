Entry for the make.fun bounty [Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/c/u-alfasurya91-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-6ed40225?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=make-the-judge-laugh-alfasurya).

`main.py` writes the one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My laptop restarted for updates on slide 3 of 47. It was the only attendee honest enough to leave.

The line was picked by duelling twenty-plus drafts against the
competition's published judge brief and the live field's strongest
lines in both orders (full drafts, field scan and duel record in
[candidates.md](candidates.md)). The field stampeded into a few lanes —
of 240 finished lines read first-hand from a field of ~1,700, roughly
30 are printer-jams-on-urgent-documents, ~20 are meetings about
meetings, ~15 are office-plant growth plans — so this entry plays an
empty lane instead: a forced update restart mid-presentation, pinned
to a concrete trace ("slide 3 of 47") and closed by personifying the
laptop as the only attendee honest enough to leave. Against the five
strongest sampled lines it wins or splits four of five head-to-heads;
the honest record, including the one line it loses to, is in
candidates.md.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
